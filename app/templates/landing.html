VendorTrust

AI-powered protection against vendor payment fraud — with immutable versioning, out-of-band verification, and strict two-person approval.

Core Principle: An invoice may request a new PayPal recipient — but it can never authorize that change. Every recipient is resolved exclusively from a cryptographically-bound, independently-approved immutable vendor version.
🤖
AI Extraction
Gemini models scan invoices for payee change proposals. Output is advisory — never authoritative.
🔒
Prompt Injection Defense
Adversarial override instructions are automatically detected and force a human-review hold.
✉️
Out-of-Band Verification
HMAC-keyed OTP delivered to the pre-established trusted vendor contact — never from the invoice itself.
👥
Two-Person Approval
Requester and Approver are permanently separated. Neither role can self-approve.
📌
Immutable Versioning
Historical vendor records are append-only. Database triggers prevent modification or deletion.
💳
Strict Payout API
Accepts only a payment_intent_id. Recipient is always resolved from the immutable approved version.
